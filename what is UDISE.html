What is UDISE+ & What Does It Track?
  • National EMIS Platform: Central EMIS managed by the Ministry of Education, covering 14.5+ lakh schools, 24+ crore students, and 1 crore teachers.
  • Student Tracking (SDMS): Digital child-wise unit-level profiling, demographic categories, BPL status, CWSN aids, and APAAR IDs.
  • Infrastructure & Facilities: Tracks classrooms, drinking water, functional toilets for boys/girls, ICT labs, ramps, and electricity.
  • Permanent 11-Digit Code: Geo-tagged authentic national identifier for every recognized and unrecognized school.
Why Was UDISE+ Created?
  • Eliminating Paper Lag: Transitioned from delayed annual paper Data Capture Formats (DCF) to instant, real-time digital entry.
  • Evidence-Based Policy: Enables precise budget allocation and infrastructure grants (Samagra Shiksha, PM SHRI).
  • Dropout Prevention: Tracks critical grade transition points to identify out-of-school children and retain students.
  • Authentic Beneficiary Delivery: Ensures direct benefits (PM POSHAN, uniforms, scholarships) reach genuine students without duplication.
