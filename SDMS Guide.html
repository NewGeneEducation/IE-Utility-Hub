Student Database Management System (SDMS)

Child-Wise Tracking, System Operations & Troubleshooting Guide

Operational Architecture
Why SDMS Was Created
  • Unit-Level Child Profiling: Replaced aggregate schoolhead counts with individual digital records carrying a Permanent Education Number (PEN).
  • Direct Benefit Tracking: Ensures entitlements (scholarships, uniforms, textbooks, PM POSHAN) reach verified beneficiaries directly.
  • Elimination of Ghost Admissions: Cross-validates demographic details to remove fraudulent or duplicate registrations across institutions.
  • Tracking Migration: Seamlessly transfers student records across schools and states when families migrate.
How to Use SDMS in 4 Steps
  • 1. Student Movement & Progression: Promote last year's students to their next grades or mark transfers-out.
  • 2. Fresh Admissions & Import: Register new nursery/Class 1 admissions or import transfers via PEN.
  • 3. Fill 3 Sub-Profiles: Complete General Profile (GP), Enrollment Profile (EP), and Facility Profile (FP).
  • 4. Validation & Certification: Generate APAAR IDs, verify demographics, and lock data with School Certification.
Student Sub-Profiles Checklist (Mandatory)
  • General Profile (GP): Full Name (as per official certificates), Gender, DOB, Mother's & Father's Name, Social Category (SC/ST/OBC/General), Minority Group, and BPL status.
  • Enrollment Profile (EP): Admission Number, Date of Admission, Medium of Instruction (MOI), Academic Stream (Classes 11-12), and Vocational Course details (if applicable).
  • Facility Profile (FP): Free Textbooks, Uniforms, Transport facilities, Scholarships (Central/State), CWSN special impairment aids, and digital device access.
Common Validation Errors & Resolutions
  • Duplicate Records / Cross-School Enrollments: If a student's record already exists in another school, request the previous institution to issue an online Transfer/Release to the National Dropbox rather than creating a fresh registration.
  • Invalid Stream or MOI: Ensure student's selected Medium of Instruction and Academic Stream match the approved offerings configured in the Master School Profile.
  • Mandatory Biometric Update (MBU): Students turning 5 or 15 must complete their biometric update at an authorized enrollment center to prevent record deactivation.

Student Movement & Progression Sub-Menus
  • Progression Activity: Mandatory start-of-year task to promote, retain, or mark previous session students as transferred-out before updating classes.
  • Import Module: Used to admit transfer-in students using their 11-digit Permanent Education Number (PEN) and Date of Birth (DOB).
  • Send to Dropbox: Releases students who left your school into the common national pool so their next school can import them.
  • Inactivate Student Module: Permanently deactivates deceased, out-of-system, or duplicate student records with formal justification.
  • Dropbox Student List: Audit roster tracking whether released students have been admitted elsewhere or remain un-enrolled.
Search, Audit & Compliance Sub-Menus
  • Global Student Search: Locates a child's PEN nationwide across states and boards using mobile number or basic demographic criteria.
  • Track Student: Traces the child's historical movement trail, showing past schools, academic years, and grades.
  • Form Fields Validation Guide: Technical dictionary specifying character constraints, acceptable date formats, and mandatory rules.
  • Student Aadhaar Availability: Compliance queue filtering verified records, failed validations needing correction, and pending submissions.
  • Notifications: Official alert feed for circulars, portal maintenance schedules, and district/state compliance deadlines.
Common Student Import Issues & Fixes
  • Active in Previous School: The previous school must mark the student as "Left School with TC" in their Progression Module to release them to the Dropbox.
  • Progression Not Finalized: If the previous school hasn't certified its annual progression, individual records remain locked for external import.
  • PEN / DOB Typo: Run a Global Student Search to verify the exact registered Date of Birth and PEN spelling.
  • Inter-State Transfers: Select the dedicated "Import Outside State" tool for students moving from another state/UT.
Class Shift & "No Data Found" Fixes
  • "No Data Found" Dropdown Trap: Always reset all filter dropdowns (Class, Section, Status) to "All" and clear search terms.
  • Target Class/Section Unavailable: Ensure the section is created under School Details → Section Management before shifting.
  • Incomplete Student Profile: Complete and save all 3 forms (GP → EP → FP) to Completed status before shifting.
  • Locked Certification: If the school has certified the session, request the Block/District MIS coordinator to temporarily unlock data entry.
Student Release Request Management

Use this module when a student is currently enrolled in another school and needs to be admitted to your school without creating duplicate admissions.

  • Within State: Used for students transferring from another recognized school located within the same state/UT by searching their PEN.
  • Outside State: Used for students migrating from another state or union territory across India to route cross-state transfer requests.
  • Generate Release Request: Receiving school enters the child's PEN and Date of Birth to trigger the electronic transfer request.
  • Approve Release Request(s): Originating/previous school reviews pending transfer requests, checks clearance/TC, and approves release.
  • Inbox & Sent Queues: Inbox tracks incoming release requests from other schools, while Sent tracks your outgoing requests (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
  • Key Rule: A student cannot be imported or enrolled into your active roster until the originating school officially approves the release request in their portal.
Notifications Console & Alerts

Official real-time administrative communications console from Ministry of Education, State Project Directorates, DEO, and BEO offices.

  • Deadline Circulars & Timelines: Alerts regarding mandatory submission deadlines for Data Capture Formats (DCF), progression, and final school certification.
  • System & Maintenance Updates: Advance announcements regarding scheduled portal downtimes, server maintenance, or deployment of new sub-modules (APAAR/MBU).
  • State & District Directives: Official circulars, operational guidelines, and error rectification notices issued by higher education authorities.
  • Validation Warnings & Rejection Flags: Automated alerts flagging pending biometric updates (MBU), unlinked identification cases, or duplicate enrollment warnings.
  • "No Notification" Status: Indicates all previous circulars have been archived and no critical compliance actions are currently pending.
