Step-by-Step Guide: Downloading Active Students Excel from UDISE+

Instructions:
  1. Log in to your UDISE+ SDMS Portal using your School / College credentials.
  2. In the left-hand navigation sidebar, click on List of All Students → Active Students.
  3. Click the green "Download Excel" button located at the top-right corner.
  4. Upload the exact downloaded file directly into this dashboard without opening or modifying any columns.
